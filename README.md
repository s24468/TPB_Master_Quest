# TPB Master Quest

> **Status:** In Development (Alpha)
> **Platform:** PC
> **Engine:** Unity 6

## Overview

**TPB Master Quest** is an immersive single-player adventure blending **Third-Person Exploration** with a deep, strategic **Collectible Card Game (CCG)** called *TPB Strike*. Set in a calm, fascinating fantasy world, the game follows your journey from an aspiring novice to the ultimate *TPB Strike Master*. Traverse through scenic routes, visit bustling village menus, defeat challenging opponents, and build the ultimate deck to conquer regional tournaments.

---

## Key Features

* **Hybrid Gameplay Loop:** Combine free-roaming third-person exploration across dynamic routes with tactical, turn-based card battles.
* **TPB Strike Card System:** Collect, buy, trade, and customize your deck. Utilize cards featuring unique attributes like Mana Cost, Power Levels (T, P, B), and special abilities.
* **Level-Based Progression:** Travel sequentially through structured areas—moving from 2D UI village hubs to 3D exploration routes filled with challengers, traders, and hidden treasures.
* **Player-Driven Pace:** Choose your playstyle—speed-run through tournaments with high-risk tactics or take your time training, exploring, and building a flawless collection.
* **Narrative-Driven Quest:** Rise through the ranks by collecting rare zone cards and winning prestigious regional tournaments.

## Inspiration & Market Positioning

Drawing inspiration from classics like *Pokémon Emerald*, *Hearthstone*, *Roguebook*, and *Bakugan Battle Brawlers*, **TPB Master Quest** stands out through its unique **Narrative Integration** bridging the gap between traditional RPG world-exploration and deep card battler mechanics without the heavy reliance on pure multiplayer RNG or overcomplicated rulebooks.
