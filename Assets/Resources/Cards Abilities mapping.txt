A1 -> draw a card  
A2 -> draw two cards
B1 -> put the top card of your deck into your mana
B2 -> put the two top cards of your deck into your mana
C1 -> opponent discards one random card from hand
C2 -> opponent discards two random cards from hand
C3 -> opponent discards three random cards from hand
D1 -> you discard one card at random from hand
D2 -> you discard two cards at random from hand
D3 -> you discard three cards at random from hand
